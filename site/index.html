ADocument Quality
The submitted document cannot be accepted due to format, legibility, or validity issues.
Examples
Screenshot or phone photo instead of a scanned PDF · Blurry or dark scan where text is unreadable · Expired document (passport, license, registration cert) · Black-and-white copy when color is required
Request
"Please resubmit [document name] as a clear, color PDF scan. The document must be valid and unexpired, with all text clearly legible."
BDocument Completeness
The required document exists but is not fully submitted.
Examples
Signature page missing from incorporation certificate · Pages 3–5 missing from bank statement · Form submitted with required sections left blank
Request
"Please resubmit the complete [document name]. The following is missing: [specify page / section / signature]."
CTranslation Required
Document is in a language other than English or Traditional Chinese and requires a certified translation.
Examples
Mandarin-only incorporation certificate · Arabic bank statement · Japanese company profile or registry extract
Request
"Please submit a certified English translation of [document], signed and stamped by a licensed translator. Submit the original and translation together."
DBeneficial Ownership / UBO
Beneficial ownership structure cannot be confirmed from submitted documents. All natural persons holding >25% must be identified and verified.
Examples
Corporate shareholder with no natural persons identified below · UBO holding >25% not declared · Identity documents (passport or national ID) missing for one or more UBOs
Request
"Please submit: (1) UBO declaration form naming all natural persons with >25% beneficial ownership; (2) Passport or national ID copy for each identified UBO."
ESource of Funds / Source of Wealth
The origin of funds (SoF) or accumulated wealth (SoW) cannot be verified. SoF = where the transaction funds came from. SoW = how the UBO accumulated their overall wealth. Both may be required for high-volume or complex cases.
Examples
Bank statement insufficient for declared transaction volume · Unexplained large inflows · No business income documentation · Unusual net worth relative to stated business activities
Request
"Please provide bank statements (6–12 months) and documentation of business income — audited financials, tax returns, or invoices. For SoW: a written statement with supporting evidence (property records, investment statements)."
FBusiness Legitimacy
Stated business activities cannot be verified or are inconsistent with submitted documentation.
Examples
Business license scope does not match stated activities · Website inaccessible or describes different services · Trading volume claimed is inconsistent with business size or history
Request
"Please provide: business license (showing permitted activities), company website URL, and a brief description of your primary business activities and customers or suppliers."
GAdverse Findings
Screening has returned a match or finding that requires compliance review before any further action.
Examples
PEP (Politically Exposed Person) match on director or UBO · OFAC / UN / local sanctions list match · Adverse media linking a principal to regulatory action, fraud, or financial crime
⚠ Do NOT request documents from the customer for Category G findings. All actions must be directed by SG Compliance. This case may require EDD. Escalate immediately and do not advance state without Compliance instruction.
HInternal / Process Issue
The delay is caused by an internal issue — not by the customer.
Examples
Reviewer unavailable due to leave · Unclear internal ownership · Pending legal or compliance opinion · System access issue
⚠ Do NOT send the case to Waiting Customer for Blocker H. Case stays in the reviewer's current state. Log the issue in Activity Log and notify Ops PM. The customer is not responsible for internal delays and must not be contacted about them.
