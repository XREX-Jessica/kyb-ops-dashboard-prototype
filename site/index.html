EDD is a Compliance decision — KYB can flag concerns but cannot trigger EDD. Not every G finding requires EDD: a false positive resolved quickly does not. EDD can revert to Compliance Review if elevated risk is cleared, with documented rationale in Activity Log.
ADocument Quality
Submitted documents are not suitable for review due to quality issues.
Typical Examples
Black-and-white copy · Screenshot · Cropped image · Blurry document · Reflection / glare · Low resolution
BDocument Completeness
Required documents or required information are missing.
Typical Examples
Missing company document · Missing declaration · Missing signature · Missing shareholder register · Missing required fields
CTranslation Required
Submitted documents are not in English and require translation or certified translation.
Typical Examples
Vietnamese company registration · Thai corporate documents · Chinese constitutional documents
DOwnership Transparency
Ownership structure or beneficial ownership cannot be clearly determined.
Typical Examples
UBO unclear · Ownership chart missing · Shareholding structure incomplete · Shareholder information insufficient
ESource of Funds / Source of Wealth
Source of funds or source of wealth cannot be sufficiently validated.
Typical Examples
Financial statements requested · Bank statements requested · UBO wealth explanation requested · Source of wealth clarification required
Note
SOF and SOW are intentionally grouped together in V1 and may be separated in future versions.
⚠ Specify SoF / SoW / Both in the state change modal when entering Waiting Customer. The system requires a sub-selection before confirming.
FBusiness Activity Verification
Business operations or commercial activity cannot be sufficiently validated.
Typical Examples
Invoice requested · Purchase order requested · Bill of lading requested · Shipping documents requested · Customer or supplier contract requested · Business website review
GRegulatory / Risk Concern
Potential regulatory, compliance, or risk concerns require further assessment.
Typical Examples
Adverse media · PEP exposure · Sanctions concern · High-risk jurisdiction · Elevated internal risk indicators
Note
A Regulatory / Risk Concern does not automatically trigger EDD. Additional assessment may be required.
⚠ G as the sole blocker blocks the Waiting Customer transition. G + document blockers is allowed.
HInternal Process Issue
The case is delayed due to internal operational or system-related issues.
Typical Examples
Reviewer assignment pending · Internal handoff delay · System issue · Missing internal follow-up
⚠ Do NOT send the case to Waiting Customer for Blocker H. Case stays in the reviewer's current state. Log in Activity Log and notify Ops PM.
Selina Chang — RM
Responsibilities
  • Create KYB cases at point of customer registration
  • Maintain customer relationship throughout the onboarding lifecycle
  • Log every customer contact attempt with date and outcome
  • Set and update Next Follow-up Date after each contact
  • Tick Customer Responded immediately when customer submits documents
  • Advance KYB Form Pending → KYB Submitted after confirming document submission
  • Mark Abandoned when case is inactive for a defined period, customer explicitly withdraws, or RM confirms termination
Does NOT
  • Assess or review KYB documents — Arianna's scope
  • Change state to KYB Review, Compliance Review, or EDD
  • Remove the Customer Responded checkbox — reviewer's action
  • Contact customer about compliance findings — SG Compliance directs this
Arianna — KYB Reviewer
Responsibilities
  • Pick up KYB Submitted cases within 1 business day
  • Change state to KYB Review explicitly before starting review
  • Identify ALL blockers in a single review cycle
  • Log all blockers with specific notes before entering Waiting Customer
  • Pick up from Returned from Customer queue within 4 business hours
  • Clear the Customer Responded checkbox when picking up a returned case
  • Advance to Compliance Review only when all KYB criteria are fully met
Does NOT
  • Contact customers directly — Selina's scope
  • Make decisions on sanctions, PEP, or adverse media — escalate to SG Compliance
  • Approve or reject cases
SG Compliance
Responsibilities
  • Pick up Compliance Review cases within 1 business day
  • Conduct PEP screening, sanctions screening, and adverse media review
  • Assess source of funds and source of wealth as required
  • Trigger Waiting Customer (Triggered By: Compliance) when additional documents are needed
  • Trigger EDD when specific risk criteria are met
  • Issue Approved or Rejected with findings documented in Activity Log before state change
  • Manage EDD cases through to a final decision
Does NOT
  • Review KYB-layer documents — Arianna's scope
  • Contact customers directly — Selina's scope
  • Issue Approved or Rejected without a logged finding
